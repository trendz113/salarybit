Prepay the home loan, or invest the extra?

Both options use the same amount of money and the same number of years, so it's a fair comparison. Paying extra to the bank is a sure saving — you know exactly how much interest you save. Putting money in the stock market is not sure — it can go up, but it can also go down, like the chart above shows. Only the "interest you save" number is guaranteed.
