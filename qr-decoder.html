The problem: portals want the raw string, not your PDF
e-Khata, BBMP, and BBSCC documents carry a QR code that encodes a PKCS#7 digital signature — something like 2933699+++MIIB/AYJKoZIhvc.... When you're verifying a property before purchase, processing a bank loan, submitting to Kaveri Online, or fighting a legal dispute over document authenticity, the system on the other end usually asks you to paste that exact string — not upload the PDF, not send a screenshot. If you can't produce it, the verification step stalls.
📵
Why normal QR scanner apps fail here
This isn't a URL-style QR made for phone cameras. It's a dense, high-density data QR embedded as a small raster image inside the PDF page — often under-resolved once the page is flattened. Google Lens and most scanner apps either say "no QR found" or return garbled, truncated text on it.

Common Questions

What is the QR string used for? +
The QR string (e.g. 2933699+++MIIB/AYJKoZIhvc...) is a PKCS#7 digital signature embedded in government documents like e-Khata. It is used to verify document authenticity and is required by certain legal and banking processes.
Which PDFs does this work on? +
This tool works on e-Khata documents issued by BBMP, BBSCC, and other Karnataka municipal corporations. It supports any government PDF with embedded image-based QR codes.
Is my document data safe? +
Your PDF is processed in memory only and is never stored on any server. Once the QR string is extracted and returned to you, the file is discarded immediately.
