EPF withdrawal process India 2026

The Employee Provident Fund (EPF), launched by the Government of India in 1952, is a contributory pension scheme that aims to provide a steady income to employees after their retirement. EPF withdrawal is a process that allows you to access your accumulated EPF amount in case of an emergency or after resignation. In this article, we will discuss the EPF withdrawal process in India, its rules, and procedures. **EPF Withdrawal Rules** Before we proceed with the EPF withdrawal process, it's essential to understand the rules and conditions governing EPF withdrawals. As per the EPF Scheme 1952, you are eligible to withdraw your EPF amount in the following situations: * **Resignation or termination**: If you leave your job due to resignation or termination, you can withdraw your EPF amount. * **Retirement**: You can withdraw your EPF amount after retirement. * **Death**: In case of death, your EPF amount will be paid to your nominee or guardian. * **Medical emergency**: You can withdraw your EPF amount due to a medical emergency. **Document Required for EPF Withdrawal** To withdraw your EPF amount, you need to submit the following documents: * **EPF membership number**: Your EPF membership number, also known as the UAN (Universal Account Number), is required to initiate the withdrawal process. * **Identity proof**: You need to submit your identity proof, such as a Aadhaar card, PAN card, or Passport. * **Address proof**: You need to submit your address proof, such as a Aadhaar card, driving license, or utility bills. * **Passport-sized photograph**: You need to attach a recent passport-sized photograph. * **Self-attested photograph**: You need to attach a self-attested photograph of your employer's manager/authorised official. **EPF Withdrawal Process** The EPF withdrawal process is as follows: 1. **Get your UAN**: If you don't have a UAN, you need to activate it by submitting an application to the EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation). 2. **Log in to the UAN portal**: Once you have your UAN, you can log in to the UAN portal using your UAN number and password. 3. **Submit the withdrawal request**: On the UAN portal, you need to submit a withdrawal request by selecting the reason for withdrawal. 4. **Self-attest the form**: You need to self-attest the EPF withdrawal form, which can be downloaded from the EPFO website. 5. **Submit the form**: You need to submit the self-attested EPF withdrawal form to your employer. 6. **Employer verification**: Your employer needs to verify the form and submit it to the EPFO. 7. **EPFO processing**: The EPFO will process your withdrawal request and credit the amount to your bank account. **Example EPF Withdrawal Amounts** The following table shows an example of EPF
