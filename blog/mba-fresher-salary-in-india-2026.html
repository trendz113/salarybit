MBA Fresher Salary in India 2026: What to Expect?

The MBA fresher salary in India is an essential topic of discussion for many aspiring business graduates. The Indian job market is highly competitive, and salaries can vary greatly depending on several factors, including the industry, sector, location, and company size. In this article, we will provide you with a comprehensive overview of the average MBA fresher salary in India in 2026.

The salaries mentioned in this article are based on data from reputable sources, including recruitment firms, job portals, and industry reports. Keep in mind that these figures are subject to change and may not reflect your actual salary, depending on your skills, experience, and performance.

Average MBA Fresher Salary in India 2026

The average MBA fresher salary in India in 2026 ranges from ₹8.5 lakhs to ₹22.5 lakhs per annum, depending on the industry and company size. Here are some average salary ranges for MBA fresher positions in India:

Industry Average Salary (₹ lakh pa)
Finance 10.5 - 16.5
Marketing 8.5 - 13.5
Operations 9 - 15
Human Resources 7.5 - 12.5
Supply Chain 8 - 14
Consulting 12 - 20

As you can see, the average salary for MBA fresher positions varies significantly across different industries. The finance industry offers the highest average salary, followed by the consulting and operations sectors.

Top Companies Offering Highest MBA Fresher Salary in India 2026

Here are some top companies in India that offer high salaries to MBA freshers:

  • KPMG: ₹18 - ₹25 lakhs per annum
  • Deloitte: ₹16 - ₹22 lakhs per annum
  • Ericsson: ₹15 - ₹20 lakhs per annum
  • IBM: ₹14 - ₹19 lakhs per annum
  • Wipro: ₹13 - ₹18 lakhs per annum

Keep in mind that these figures are based on industry reports and may not reflect the actual salaries offered by these companies.

Conclusion

The MBA fresher salary in India is an attractive proposition for many aspiring business graduates. With salaries ranging from ₹8.5 lakhs to ₹22.5 lakhs per annum, India offers a promising career path for MBA freshers. By choosing the right industry, sector, and company, you can maximize your earning potential and achieve your career goals.
