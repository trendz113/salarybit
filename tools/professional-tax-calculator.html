Professional Tax in India — Complete State-wise Guide 2026

Professional Tax (PT) is a state-level tax levied on salaried employees and professionals. It is deducted monthly from your salary and deposited with the state government. The maximum PT payable is ₹2,500 per year under the Constitution. Not all states levy PT.

State-wise Professional Tax Rates 2026

  • Maharashtra: Up to ₹7,500 gross: Nil · ₹7,501–10,000: ₹175/mo · Above ₹10,000: ₹200/mo (₹300 in Feb)
  • Karnataka: Up to ₹14,999: Nil · ₹15,000 and above: ₹200/mo (₹2,400/year)
  • Tamil Nadu: Slab-based, max ₹208/month
  • West Bengal: Up to ₹8,500: Nil · ₹8,501–10,000: ₹90/mo · up to ₹12,000: ₹110/mo · up to ₹14,999: ₹130/mo · above ₹15,000: ₹200/mo
  • Delhi, UP, Rajasthan, HP: No Professional Tax

Is Professional Tax Deductible?

Yes. Professional Tax paid is deductible under Section 16(iii) of the Income Tax Act — available under both Old and New Tax Regimes. It reduces your gross salary for tax purposes. Maximum deduction: ₹2,500 per year (the constitutional cap on PT).

Who pays Professional Tax — employer or employee?
Both. The employee's PT is deducted from salary. But the employer also pays a separate Employer PT registration fee/tax in states like Maharashtra. The employee's portion is what shows as a deduction on your payslip.
