Gratuity Calculation Formula India

Gratuity is a one-time payment made to an employee upon their retirement or resignation from the organization, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. This payment is a way of expression of employer's appreciation towards the employee's long service, dedication, and hard work. In this article, we will discuss the gratuity calculation formula in India and the rules surrounding it.

Eligibility Criteria for Gratuity

To be eligible for gratuity, an employee must meet the following criteria: * The employee must have completed at least 5 years of service with the same organization. * The employee must have served 10 years in total in the organized sector, with or without breaks, in 10 or more establishments (whether as an employee or under an employment contract). * The employee must be employed in a factory, mine, oilfield, plantation, port, railway company, or any other establishment to which the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 applies.

Gratuity Calculation Formula India

The gratuity calculation formula in India is based on the Pay of the employee and the length of their service. The formula is as follows: * 15 days' wages for each completed year of service (for the first 5 years) * 3 months' wages for each completed 6th year and beyond The wages used for gratuity calculation include only basic pay and not any allowances or increments.

Illustration of Gratuity Calculation Formula:

Let's take an example to understand the calculation better. | Year of Service | Salary (RUPEES) | Gratuity Calculation | | --- | --- | --- | | 5 years | 50,000 | 15 days' wages x 5 years = 75 days' wages | | 10 years | 55,000 | 15 days' wages x 5 years = 75 days' wages | | | | + 3 months' wages x 5 years = 375/30 = 37.5 months' wages | | | | Total: 112.5 months' wages (or 9.375 years' wages) |

Gratuity Calculation Example:

John has worked for 15 years with XYZ limited. His basic salary is 60,000 rupees per month. What will be his gratuity amount? * 15 years = 5 + 5 + 5 (5 years each) * Gratuity calculation: 1) First 5 years: 15 days' wage x 5 = 75 x 60,000 = 4,500,000 2) 5 years more: (next 5 years) = 15 days x 60,000 = 900,000 3) 5 years more: (next 5 years) = 3 months x 60,000 = 1,800,000 * Total gratuity amount = 4,500,000 + 900,000 + 1,800,000 = 7,200,000

Gratuity Payment and Limitation:

* The maximum amount of gratuity payable is Rs. 20 lakhs (20,00,000). * The payment of gratuity must be made within 30 days from the date of employee's retirement or resignation.

FAQs

To help you better understand the concept of gratuity, here are some frequently asked questions:

Frequently Asked Questions

Is gratuity payable on resignation?

Yes, gratuity is payable on resignation, provided the employee has completed at least 5 years of service.

Is gratuity taxable?

Yes, gratuity is taxable as per the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Can gratuity be claimed after retirement?

No, gratuity must be claimed before retirement, and the payment must be made within 30 days from the date of retirement.

Remember, understanding the gratuity calculation formula in India is crucial to determine your gratuity amount. Consult your HR department or a tax professional to ensure you get the correct amount.
