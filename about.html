Our Story

SalaryBit was born out of frustration. Every time we received an offer letter or got a salary hike, we had the same questions: What will my actual in-hand salary be? Which tax regime should I choose? Is my HRA exemption being calculated correctly?

Existing tools were either too complicated, full of ads, required login, or gave generic answers. We wanted something that felt like talking to a smart friend who knows Indian tax law — instant, accurate, and free.

So we built SalaryBit. A platform where any salaried Indian can walk in with their CTC, and walk out knowing exactly what their financial picture looks like.

Our Principles

  • Privacy first: We don't collect, store, or sell any personal data. All calculations happen in your browser.
  • Always free: SalaryBit will always be free to use. No paywalls, no premium tiers.
  • India-specific: Every tool is built around Indian tax laws, EPFO rules, and the Indian salary structure — not generic global calculators.
  • Kept current: We update all tools when tax rules change — budget announcements, new regime defaults, and regulatory updates are reflected promptly.
  • No login required: You should never have to create an account to understand your own salary.

Important Disclaimer

SalaryBit is an informational tool. All calculations are estimates based on the information you provide and the tax rules applicable as of FY 2026-27.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SalaryBit really free?

Yes, completely. Every tool on SalaryBit is free to use, with no login, no subscription, and no hidden charges. We are supported by non-intrusive advertising.

Do you store my salary or personal data?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not have a server that receives your salary inputs. No data is stored, sent, or sold — ever.

How accurate are the calculations?

Our calculations are based on the latest tax rules for FY 2026-27, including the New Tax Regime defaults, standard deduction of ₹75,000, and updated EPF rates. However, individual circumstances vary — professional advice is recommended for ITR filing and complex financial decisions.

How can I contact you?

You can reach us via the Contact form on the SalaryBit homepage. We typically respond within 2 business days.

I found a calculation error. How do I report it?

We take accuracy very seriously. If you spot an error, please use the Contact form on our homepage with details of the issue. We'll investigate and fix it promptly.
