Doctor Salary in Government Hospitals, India: 2026

As of May 2026, the salary structure for doctors working in government hospitals across India has seen significant changes. In this article, we'll take a closer look at the current pay scales, along with real salary numbers, to help you understand the financial landscape for medical professionals in the public sector.

Basic Pay Scales for Doctors in Government Hospitals

Government hospitals in India are governed by the National Health Mission (NHM) and adhere to the pay scales set by the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. According to the latest revision in 2025, doctors are classified into different pay scales based on their qualifications, experience, and position.

Take-Home Salary for Doctors

The salary numbers mentioned above are the basic pay scales. However, the take-home salary for doctors would be lower due to deductions for income tax, provident fund, and other social security contributions. Assuming a tax slab of 20% and a deduction rate of 12% for provident fund, the estimated take-home salary for doctors in May 2026 would be:

Conclusion
