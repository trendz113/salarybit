Gratuity calculation formula India

In India, gratuity is a type of retirement benefit provided to employees by their employers. It is generally paid to eligible employees at the time of retirement, resignation, or death. The gratuity amount is calculated based on the employee's last drawn salary, length of service, and the applicable gratuity rate. ### Eligibility Criteria for Gratuity in India To be eligible for gratuity, an employee must have completed at least 5 years of continuous service with the same employer. If an employee leaves the company without completing 5 years, they may still be eligible for gratuity if they have completed 1 year of service and are terminated due to redundancy, closure of the company, or sickness. ### How to Calculate Gratuity in India? The gratuity amount in India is calculated based on the employee's last drawn salary, length of service, and the applicable gratuity rate. The gratuity amount can be calculated using the following formula: **Gratuity = (15 days' wages × number of years of service)** Where: * 15 days' wages are calculated based on the employee's average daily wages in the last 10 days of the month. * Number of years of service is the total number of years an employee has spent with the same employer. ### Calculation of 15 Days' Wages To calculate the gratuity amount, the employee's last drawn salary is divided by 30 to arrive at the average daily wages, and then multiplied by 15. **Example:** Ramesh has a monthly salary of ₹50,000. His average daily wages in the last 10 days of the month is calculated as follows: €50,000 (monthly salary) / 30 (average number of working days per month) = €1,667 (average daily wages) €1,667 (average daily wages) × 15 (days) = €25,000 (15 days' wages) ### Calculation of Gratuity Amount Based on the above calculation, Ramesh's 15 days' wages amount to ₹25,000. To calculate the gratuity amount, the number of years of service is multiplied by the 15 days' wages. **Example:** Ramesh has completed 5 years of service with the same employer. His gratuity amount will be calculated as follows: €25,000 (15 days' wages) × 5 (years of service) = €125,000 (gratuity amount) ### Gratuity Rates in India The gratuity rate in India is 15 days' wages per year of service, for the first 5 years of service. For each additional year of service beyond 5, the gratuity rate increases by 8.33% for each year. **Example:** Suppose a person leaves the company after serving 10 years. Their total gratuity would be (10 x 15 days wages) but it would be increased by 16.66% after five years of service. This increase can be calculated as 10 x (8.33%).
