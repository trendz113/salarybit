Gratuity calculation formula India

What is Gratuity in India?

In India, gratuity is a form of terminal benefit that an employee receives from their employer upon retirement, resignation, or death. It is a way for employers to show appreciation for an employee's service and dedication to the organization. Gratuity is usually calculated based on the employee's last drawn salary and the number of years they have served with the company.

Gratuity Calculation Formula India

The gratuity calculation formula in India is based on the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. According to this act, the gratuity payable to an employee is calculated based on their last drawn salary and the number of years they have served with the employer. The formula for calculating gratuity is as follows: Gratuity = (15 days' salary for each year of service) x Total number of years of service

Gratuity Calculation Example

Let's consider an example to understand how gratuity is calculated. Suppose an employee has served with a company for 10 years and their last drawn salary is Rs. 50,000 per month. | Month | Total Salary | | --- | --- | | 6 | 300,000 | | 12 | 600,000 | | 18 | 900,000 | | 24 | 1,200,000 | | 30 | 1,500,000 | | 1 | 300,000 | The total salary drawn by the employee over the 10 years is Rs. 14,700,000. To calculate gratuity, we multiply this amount by 15 days' salary for each year of service. Gratuity = (15 days' salary for each year of service) x 10 years = (15/20) x 10 x 50,000 = 6 x 10 x 2,50,000 = 15,00,000 So, in this example, the gratuity payable to the employee would be Rs. 15,00,000.

NB: Some Special Cases

It's worth noting that there are some special cases where the gratuity payable to an employee may be different from the above calculation. * When the employee's last drawn salary is greater than 50,000 rupees per month, the gratuity payable will be 16 days' salary for each year of service instead of 15 days. * When the total period of service extends beyond the end of a half year, the gratuity will be calculated with reference to half year. * When an employee has worked in an office or establishment that has been closed down or is no longer in existence, gratuity will be paid within 6 months from the date of closure.

Gratitude and Conclusion

In conclusion, the gratuity calculation formula in India is a simple yet effective way to appreciate an employee's service and dedication to the organization. By understanding this formula, employees can determine the gratuity they are eligible to receive based on their last drawn salary and the number of years they have served with their employer. By understanding the rules and regulations surrounding gratuity, employees and employers alike can better understand their rights and obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gratuity in India?

Gratuity is a form of terminal benefit that an employee receives from their employer upon retirement, resignation, or death.

How is gratuity calculated in India?

The gratuity is calculated by multiplying the last drawn salary by 15 days' salary for each year of service.

What is the difference between gratuity and retirement benefits?

Gratuity is a form of terminal benefit that is paid to an employee upon retirement, resignation, or death, whereas retirement benefits are a type of benefit that is paid to an employee during their retirement.

Can gratuity be claimed from a former employer?

Yes, an employee can claim gratuity from their former employer upon leaving the job.
