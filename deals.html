DJI Osmo Pocket 3

Best for: Vlogging, product videos, Instagram ReelsPrice range: ₹41,000 – ₹52,000

This is the closest thing to a "just press record and it looks professional" camera — the built-in 3-axis gimbal handles stabilization better than anything you'd get from a phone mount. Worth it once you're serious about video, not for a first ₹0-budget experiment. One honest limitation: battery life is short on long shoot days, so a second battery is a near-mandatory add-on.

Boya BY-M1 Lavalier Microphone

Best for: Clear voiceovers, interviews, talking-head videosPrice range: ₹500 – ₹900

Audio quality matters more than video quality for viewer retention, and this clip-on mic fixes the single biggest giveaway of "shot on a phone" content — tinny, echoey sound. It's wired, not wireless, which is the honest trade-off at this price — but for sit-down recording (podcasts, tutorials, talking-to-camera) that's rarely a real limitation.

10-inch LED Ring Light with Tripod Stand

Best for: Instagram Reels, at-home product shoots, video callsPrice range: ₹800 – ₹1,800

The fastest, cheapest upgrade to how your videos look — even footage from an entry-level phone looks noticeably better with even, flattering light instead of whatever your ceiling bulb is doing. Buy one with an adjustable colour temperature dial; the fixed-white ones can wash out darker skin tones and look harsh on camera.
