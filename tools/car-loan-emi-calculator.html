Car Loan in India — 2026 Guide

A car loan (auto loan) is a secured loan where the car itself serves as collateral. Banks and NBFCs offer up to 85–90% financing of on-road price. Your EMI depends on loan amount, interest rate, and tenure.

True Cost of Car Ownership

The sticker price is just the beginning. Add: Registration and RTO charges (9–18% of ex-showroom depending on state), insurance (1–3% p.a.), fuel (₹5–8/km for petrol), maintenance (₹15,000–30,000/year), and depreciation (15% first year, 10–15% subsequent years under Income Tax rules).

What is the ideal car EMI as a % of salary?
Financial planners recommend keeping your car EMI under 10–15% of your net take-home salary. A higher ratio strains your cash flow and limits savings. Rule of thumb: Car on-road price should not exceed 6 months of your gross annual salary.
Is there tax benefit on car loan EMI?
No tax benefit is available on car loans for personal vehicles. However, if you use the car for business purposes and are self-employed or have a proprietorship, you can claim depreciation and interest as business expenses. Salaried employees cannot claim any tax deduction on car loan EMI.
