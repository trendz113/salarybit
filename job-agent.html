FAQ

Common questions.

Does the agent actually apply on my behalf? +
Yes. Once you submit your profile, the agent scans LinkedIn Jobs every morning at 10 AM and automatically applies to roles matching your criteria. Every application is logged in your personal dashboard.
When will I get my first update? +
Your agent activates within 24 hours. From then on, you'll receive a daily email and Telegram message at 10 AM with a full list of jobs applied, companies contacted, and any recruiter responses.
How will I know what jobs were applied to? +
Every day at 10 AM you receive a full report listing every company, role, date, and link. Your personal dashboard at dashboard.html shows the complete history with application statuses.
Is my resume data safe? +
Absolutely. We only store a link to your resume (Google Drive or similar). Your actual resume stays in your own cloud storage — we never download or store it on our servers.
Is it really free? What's the catch? +
Yes, 100% free during early access. We're building the product and want real users giving us feedback. When we launch Pro features (AI resume customisation, cover letters, multi-platform), those will be paid. Free tier stays free.
Can I pause or stop the agent? +
Yes. Email us anytime at support@salarybit.in and we'll pause or deactivate your agent immediately. No lock-in, no questions asked.
