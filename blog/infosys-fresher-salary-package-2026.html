Factors Influencing Salary in Infosys for Freshers 2026

The Infosys fresher salary package 2026 can be influenced by various factors, including:

  • Role: Different roles come with different salary packages. For example, Software Engineer, Data Scientist, and IT Project Manager have varying salary packages.
  • Location: Freshers joining Infosys at different locations may receive varying salaries.
  • Qualifications: Freshers with higher qualifications, such as Bachelor's or Master's degrees, may receive higher salaries.

FAQs

Q: What is the minimum qualification required to join Infosys?

A: To join Infosys, freshers must have a Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 60% marks or a Master's degree with a minimum of 50% marks.

Q: Can I join Infosys after dropping out of college? No, you cannot directly join Infosys after dropping out of college. Infosys requires candidates to have a minimum education qualification and sometimes relevant work experience.

Q: How long does the recruitment process at Infosys usually take?

A: The recruitment process at Infosys can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks, depending on the role, qualifications, and performance in the assessment rounds.
