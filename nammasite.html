Frequently asked questions
Do I need to know computers or coding?
Not at all. You just WhatsApp us your product list, prices and any photos. We build everything. You only review the final site on your phone and say yes or no.
How do customers order from my website?
They browse your products, tap "Order on WhatsApp" — and WhatsApp opens automatically with the product name, quantity and their requirements already typed. You just reply with price confirmation and they place the order. Zero extra work for you.
What is Razorpay? Is it safe for my customers?
Razorpay is India's most trusted payment gateway — used by Amazon, Swiggy, Zomato and 8 lakh businesses. Customers can pay via UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, debit/credit cards. Money transfers to your bank account within 1–2 days. 100% safe and RBI approved.
Do I pay monthly for hosting?
No monthly hosting fees. We host your site on Netlify's free tier — it's completely free and handles thousands of visitors per month. You only pay the one-time build fee. Optional: buy a custom domain like yourshop.in for ₹800/year from GoDaddy or BigRock.
What if I want to add new products later?
With our ₹1,500/month retainer we handle all updates for you — add products, change prices, update photos. Or WhatsApp us anytime for a one-time update at ₹300–500 per change.
Can I see a sample website before I pay?
Yes! We built a complete demo site for a Hosakote printing shop. WhatsApp us and we'll send you the live link to preview on your phone right now — before you pay a single rupee.
What types of shops have you built for?
Printing shops, medical stores, restaurants, boutiques, coaching classes, hardware shops, electricians, carpenters — any local business with products or services to sell. If you're not sure if we can build for your business, just WhatsApp us and ask!
