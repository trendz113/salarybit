Gratuity calculation formula India

Gratuity is a payment made by an employer to an employee when they leave the company, usually after retirement or resignation. In India, the gratuity payment is governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. This act provides for the payment of gratuity to an employee who has rendered continuous service for at least five years. **Eligibility Criteria** To be eligible for gratuity, an employee must meet the following criteria: * The employee must have rendered continuous service for at least five years. * The employee must be a government servant or a private sector employee. * The employee must have a wage not exceeding Rs. 10 lakhs per month. * The employee must be a resident of India. **Gratuity Calculation Formula** The gratuity calculation formula in India is as follows: Gratuity = (15 days' wages × number of years of service) Where, * 15 days' wages = last drawn salary × 15/26 (26 being the number of days in a month) For example, if an employee has rendered 20 years of service and their last drawn salary is Rs. 50,000 per month, the gratuity would be: Gratuity = (15 days' wages × 20) = (50,000 × 15/26 × 20) = Rs. 7,69,230 Here is a table showing the gratuity calculation for different number of years of service and last drawn salary: | Last Drawn Salary (per month) | Number of Years of Service | Gratuity | | --- | --- | --- | | Rs. 30,000 | 10 | Rs. 3,23,400 | | Rs. 50,000 | 15 | Rs. 5,95,625 | | Rs. 70,000 | 20 | Rs. 8,67,850 | | Rs. 90,000 | 25 | Rs. 11,40,075 | **Payment of Gratuity** The gratuity payment must be made within 30 days from the date of termination of employment. The payment can be made by: * Cash * Cheque * Bank transfer **Important Provisions** * An employee who has opted for VRS (Voluntary Retirement Scheme) can also receive gratuity. * An employee who has been appointed to a government post through a competitive examination can receive gratuity after rendering continuous service for at least 10 years. * An employee who has been employed in India for at least 5 years and has rendered continuous service for at least 5 years can receive gratuity. **Taxation** The gratuity payment is taxable under the Income-tax Act, 1961. However, the employer can deduct an amount equal to 1.25 times the gratuity from the income of the employee and credit the same to the employee's gratuity fund. **Exemption** The gratuity payment is exempt from tax under Section 10(10) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, provided the employee has rendered continuous service for at least 5 years. **Frequently Asked Questions**
