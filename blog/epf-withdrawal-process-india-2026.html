EPF withdrawal process India 2026

In India, the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a social security scheme that helps employees save for their retirement. EPF contributions are made by both employers and employees, and the funds are invested to generate returns. As of June 2026, EPF withdrawal rules have changed, and salaried individuals must understand the process to avoid penalties or fines. **EPF Withdrawal Eligibility and Rules** Before withdrawing EPF funds, there are certain eligibility criteria and rules that apply: 1. **Minimum Service Period**: The employee must have completed at least 1 year of service with the current or previous employer to be eligible for EPF withdrawal. 2. **Age**: The employee can withdraw EPF funds at the age of 58 years, subject to certain conditions. 3. **Withdrawal Types**: There are two types of EPF withdrawals: Advance EPF Withdrawal and Final Settlement (also known as EPF Withdrawal on retirement or resignation). 4. **TDS**: A 30% TDS is deducted from the EPF withdrawal amount, subject to certain conditions. **EPF Advance Withdrawal (Partial Withdrawal)** In some cases, salaried employees may need to withdraw a portion of their EPF funds, also known as an EPF Advance Withdrawal or Partial EPF Withdrawal. This is allowed in the following situations: 1. **Medical Emergency**: In case of a medical emergency, EPF funds can be withdrawn up to 90% of the EPF balance. 2. **Home Loan Repayment**: Up to 90% of the EPF balance can be withdrawn for home loan repayment. 3. **Marriage or Higher Education**: Up to 50% of the EPF balance can be withdrawn for marriage or higher education expenses. 4. **Other Eligible Expenses**: Other eligible expenses, as determined by the EPFO, can also allow for partial EPF withdrawal. **How to Apply for EPF Advance Withdrawal** To apply for an EPF Advance Withdrawal, follow these steps: 1. **Visit EPFO Website**: Visit the Employee's Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) website and log in to your account. 2. **Apply for Withdrawal**: Fill out the application form for EPF Advance Withdrawal or Partial EPF Withdrawal. 3. **Attach Required Documents**: Attach the required documents, such as a medical certificate (in case of medical emergency) or a home loan agreement. 4. **Submit Application**: Submit the application and pay the required fees (if any). **EPF Final Settlement (Full Withdrawal)** Final Settlement, also known as EPF Withdrawal on retirement or resignation, is allowed when the employee: 1. **Leaves the Job**: Leaves the current job, either due to retirement, resignation, or retirement due to illness or disability. 2. **Closes Account**: Closes the EPF account due to retirement or resignation. 3. **Reaches Retirement Age**: Reaches the age of 58 years or more. **Table 1: EPF Contribution and Return Rates in India** | EPF Contribution Apply online through the EPFO website or through a authorized bank or EPF branch. 2. **Upload Documents**: Upload the required documents, such as proof of age, identity, and address. 3. **Pay TDS**: Pay the required TDS (if any). 4. **Verify Details**: Verify the details and confirm the withdrawal. 5. **Receive Withdrawal Amount**: Receive the EPF withdrawal amount within 10-15 working days. Failure to comply with EPF rules and regulations. 2. **Late Withdrawal**: Delayed EPF withdrawal. 3. **Incomplete Documents**: Incomplete or incorrect documents. 4. **Incorrect Details**: Incorrect or false information. **Section 10A of EPF Deduction** Section 10A of the Income-tax Act allows a tax-free withdrawal of Rs. 30,000 from the EPF account once in a lifetime. This withdrawal amount is exempt from tax, subject to certain conditions. **EPF Withdrawal Rules Post COVID-19 Lockdown** Post COVID-19 lockdown, the EPFO has relaxed some rules for EPF withdrawals, including: 1. **Increased Withdrawal Amount**: Increased the withdrawal amount for medical emergencies from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 50,000. 2. **Exemption from TDS**: Exempted the withdrawal amount from TDS (if the account holder has a low EPF balance). **Conclusion** EPF withdrawal rules have changed, and salaried employees must understand the process to avoid penalties or fines. EPF advance withdrawal, partial EPF withdrawal, and final settlement are different types of withdrawals, each with its own eligibility and rules. By following this guide, employees can easily understand the EPF withdrawal process and manage their finances effectively. **Frequently Asked Questions**

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum service period to be eligible for EPF withdrawal?

The minimum service period to be eligible for EPF withdrawal is 1 year of service with the current or previous employer.

Can I withdraw EPF funds at any age?

No, you can withdraw EPF funds at the age of 58 years, subject to certain conditions.

How much TDS is deducted from the EPF withdrawal amount?

A 30% TDS is deducted from the EPF withdrawal amount, subject to certain conditions.

What is the purpose of uploading documents when applying for EPF withdrawal?

The purpose of uploading documents is to verify the employee's identity, age, and address.

Can I apply for EPF withdrawal online?

Yes, you can apply for EPF withdrawal online through the EPFO website or through a authorized bank or EPF branch.
