Two ways to check — pick whichever fits your money habits.

1
Open the NPCI mandate portal
Visit upihelp.npci.org.in on your phone and log in with your registered mobile number + OTP.
2
Open "My Mandates"
This shows every active UPI AutoPay mandate — merchant name, amount, and which app (PhonePe/GPay/Paytm) it's linked to.
3
Confused by a merchant name?
Names like "Google" or "Arha Media and Broadcasting" don't tell you what you're paying for. Go to play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory — log into the same Google account synced on your phone — and match the amount + date. That tells you the real app.
4
Cancel right there
Back on the NPCI mandate card, tap Cancel. It'll open a QR — scan it with the UPI app shown against that mandate. Done, no merchant login needed.
This works for any subscription billed via UPI AutoPay. Takes about 5 minutes, completely free, no upload needed.

Don't want to check manually? Upload your statement and we'll do the matching for you — covers UPI, card autopay, and ACH/NACH mandates in one scan.
