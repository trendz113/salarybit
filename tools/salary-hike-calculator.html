Why Your Salary Hike Feels Smaller Than Expected

A 20% salary hike rarely translates to 20% more take-home. Here's why:

  • Progressive taxation: Higher salary pushes you into higher tax slabs — the marginal rate on the incremental income can be 20–30%
  • Higher PF: If your basic salary increases, your PF contribution (12% of basic) also increases, reducing take-home further
  • Professional Tax: Crosses into higher PT slabs in some states

The effective take-home increase is typically 12–16% on a 20% CTC hike for mid-income salaried employees.

Should I negotiate for higher CTC or higher in-hand?
Always try to negotiate for both. Ask your new employer to: (1) maximise tax-free components (NPS employer contribution 80CCD(2), meal vouchers, mobile/internet reimbursements), (2) structure salary with optimal HRA and basic, (3) offer a higher variable pay component. These structural changes can add ₹10,000–30,000/month to your take-home without increasing CTC.
