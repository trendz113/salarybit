G
Google Pay
Most widely used UPI autopay app
Easy

How to find & cancel mandates

  1. Open Google Pay app on your phone
  2. Tap your profile photo at the top right corner
  3. Scroll down — tap "Autopay" or "Manage Autopay"
  4. All active UPI mandates are listed with amounts and dates
  5. Tap any mandate you want to stop → tap "Cancel mandate"
  6. Confirm with your UPI PIN — done instantly
💡 Even if you stopped using a service, the mandate stays ACTIVE until you cancel it here. The merchant can still debit you.

🏦
Your Bank App
SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak...
Most Reliable

How to find & cancel mandates

  1. Open your bank's official app (SBI YONO, HDFC MobileBanking, iMobile etc.)
  2. Go to UPI section → look for "Manage Mandates" or "UPI Autopay"
  3. All mandates linked to that bank account appear
  4. Tap any mandate → "Revoke" or "Cancel Mandate"
  5. Approve with OTP or MPIN
💡 Bank-level cancellation is the most reliable. Even if the merchant app fails to cancel, the bank will block the debit. Always cross-check here.
⚠ Credit/debit card subscriptions do NOT appear in UPI apps. You must check your card statement separately for recurring charges.
SI
Standing Instructions (SI)
Auto-debit from savings account
Check Now
  1. Open your bank's net banking or mobile app
  2. Navigate to "My Accounts" → "Standing Instructions"
  3. Full list of recurring auto-debits appears with amounts
  4. Select any you don't need → click "Cancel / Delete SI"
  5. Confirm with OTP — cancelled instantly
CC
Credit Card Autopay
HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI Card, Amex...
  1. Open your credit card app or net banking portal
  2. Go to "Transactions" → filter by "Recurring" or last 3 months
  3. Note all repeat charges — look for ₹99, ₹149, ₹199, ₹299, ₹499, ₹599
  4. To cancel: visit the service's website and cancel from your account settings
  5. If the service refuses refund: call your bank and raise a "recurring transaction dispute"
💡 Banks can block a merchant from charging you again after you raise a dispute — use this as a last resort if a service won't let you cancel.
CR
CRED App
Best tool for credit card holders
Recommended
  1. Download CRED app and link your credit card
  2. Go to "Bills" or "Subscriptions" tab
  3. CRED automatically detects recurring charges on your card
  4. Shows each subscription with amount and last charge date
  5. Tap any subscription for step-by-step cancel instructions
💡 CRED is one of the easiest ways to see ALL your card subscriptions in one place without manually scrolling statements.

Gmail / Email Inbox Trick
Find every forgotten subscription fast
Smart Hack
  1. Open Gmail or any email app
  2. Search: subscription renewal
  3. Also search: auto-renew payment receipt
  4. Sort by sender — repeat senders = active subscriptions you forgot
  5. Click any email → visit that service's website and cancel from account settings
💡 Every service sends a renewal receipt email even if you changed payment methods. This reveals subscriptions not linked to your current phone or card.
