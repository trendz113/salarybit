FAQ

Questions answered.

Does the agent actually apply on my behalf? +
Yes. Once you submit your profile, the agent scans LinkedIn Jobs every day and automatically applies to roles matching your criteria. Every application is logged with company name, role, and date in your Google Sheets dashboard.
Why LinkedIn and not Naukri or Indeed? +
Most Indian recruiters in 2026 hire directly through LinkedIn. It's where decision makers are active, referrals happen, and top companies post first. We focus LinkedIn to get you quality interviews not just volume. Pro plan adds Naukri, Indeed and 500+ more boards.
Is my resume data safe? +
Absolutely. We only store a link to your resume (Google Drive or similar). Your actual resume stays in your own cloud storage. We never download or store your resume on our servers.
How will I know what jobs were applied to? +
Every Monday you receive a full report listing every company, role, and date applied. You also get instant email alerts the moment a recruiter responds or shortlists you.
Is it really free? What's the catch? +
Yes, 100% free during early access. We're building the product and want real users giving us feedback. When we launch Pro features (AI resume customisation, cover letters), those will be paid. Free tier stays free.
Can I pause or stop the agent? +
Yes. Email us anytime at support@salarybit.in and we'll pause or deactivate your agent immediately. No lock-in, no questions asked.
