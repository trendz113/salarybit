Frequently asked questions

How do I redeem HDFC Infinia points for flights?+

Log in to HDFC NetBanking → SmartBuy → Transfer Points → Air India Flying Returns. Transfer at 1:1 ratio. 10,000 HDFC Infinia points = 10,000 Air India miles. Business class redemptions give the best value — often 3–5× better than cashback.

How does Axis Magnus EDGE Miles work?+

Axis Magnus earns 12 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on travel and 5 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on all other spends. Transfer to Air India (1:1), InterMiles (4:5), KrisFlyer (5:4), Marriott Bonvoy (5:3). Dubai business class via InterMiles needs ~40,000–60,000 miles.

Do HDFC reward points expire?+

Yes. HDFC reward points expire 2 years from accrual (Infinia: 3 years). Check via HDFC NetBanking → Cards → Reward Points. Transfer to Air India Flying Returns via SmartBuy before they lapse — even small balances are worth saving.

Is cashback or travel redemption better?+

Travel redemption almost always wins by 2–5×. HDFC Infinia cashback = ₹0.50/point. Same points as Air India miles for Business Class = ₹1.50–₹2.50/point. 50,000 points = ₹2,500 cashback OR ₹7,500–₹12,000 in flight value.

How many Air India miles for a free flight?+

Domestic economy: 5,000–12,000 miles | Dubai economy: 20,000–30,000 | Dubai business: 45,000–60,000 | London economy: 40,000–55,000 | London business: 70,000–90,000 | New York business: 90,000–1,20,000 miles. Check airindia.in for current award charts.

How to maximise Indian credit card reward points for travel (2025 guide)

Most Indian credit cardholders redeem points for cashback or merchandise — getting roughly 0.25–0.50 paisa per point. The same points transferred to the right airline loyalty program can be worth 2–5× more. HDFC Infinia points transferred to Air India Flying Returns at a 1:1 ratio can unlock Business Class flights worth ₹40,000–₹80,000 per sector.

HDFC credit card reward points — how to use them for flights

HDFC Infinia earns 5 reward points per ₹150 spent. Points can be transferred to Air India Flying Returns at 1:1 via HDFC SmartBuy. Never redeem for merchandise — flight redemptions via Air India miles give 1.5–2.5 paisa per point, vs 0.50 paisa for cashback.

Axis Magnus EDGE Miles — India's best travel credit card explained

The Axis Magnus earns 12 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on travel and 5 EDGE Miles per ₹200 elsewhere. Transfer partners: Air India (1:1), InterMiles (4:5), KrisFlyer (5:4), Marriott Bonvoy (5:3). A cardholder spending ₹1L/month earns enough for a free Business Class ticket every 1–2 months.

Comparing cashback vs travel redemption

HDFC Infinia cashback: 1 point = ₹0.50 | 50,000 points = ₹2,500

Travel via Air India: 1 point = ₹1.50–₹2.50 | 50,000 points = ₹7,500–₹12,500 in Business Class value

Do Indian credit card reward points expire?

HDFC points: 2 years (Infinia: 3 years). Axis EDGE Miles: 3 years. Amex Membership Rewards: never expire. Air India miles expire after 36 months of no activity. Set calendar reminders every 6 months to check balances.
