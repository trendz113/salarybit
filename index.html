Privacy Policy
🔒 Last updated: April 2026

1. Who We Are

SalaryBit (salarybit.in) is a free, browser-based salary and financial calculator tool for Indian salaried employees. Our free calculators run entirely in your browser and do not send your inputs to our servers. Some optional tools (for example AI report, ITR, notice, payslip and statement tools) must send the details or documents you submit to our server and to an AI provider to produce your result; this is stated on each tool page.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

All calculations on SalaryBit are performed entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your salary figures, CTC, or any income data
  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Your tax regime preferences or deduction inputs
  • Any personally identifiable information whatsoever

Exceptions: if you use an AI or paid report tool, the information or file you submit is sent to our server and an AI service solely to generate your report. We do not use it to advertise to you. To ask for deletion of anything you submitted, email hello@salarybit.in. Payments are handled by the payment gateway; we never see or store card details.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate website usage patterns — for example, how many visitors use the tool, which sections are most popular, and geographic breakdown by region. This data is anonymised and aggregated; it does not identify you personally.

We may also use Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements. AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

SalaryBit embeds resources from Google Fonts and Cloudflare CDN to deliver fonts and JavaScript libraries. These third parties may log your IP address as part of standard web delivery. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

5. Children's Privacy

SalaryBit is intended for adults who are currently employed or planning employment. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of SalaryBit constitutes acceptance of any revised policy.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please reach us via the Contact section. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

SalaryBit makes every effort to provide accurate salary and tax computations based on publicly available Indian tax laws for FY 2026-27. However, tax rules are complex, subject to interpretation, and may change. Calculations on this platform are estimates only and should not be used as the sole basis for tax filing, salary negotiation, or financial planning.

2. Not a Substitute for Professional Advice

The results generated by SalaryBit do not constitute professional financial, tax, or legal advice. Always consult a qualified Chartered Accountant (CA), financial planner, or legal professional before making decisions based on this tool's output.

3. No Guarantee of Completeness

Indian salary structures vary widely by employer, industry, location, and contract. SalaryBit cannot account for every possible allowance, perk, stock option plan (ESOP), or employer-specific policy. Results may not match your actual in-hand salary exactly.

4. Labour Code Information

Information on the New Labour Codes (Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Social Security Code, Occupational Safety Code) is based on publicly available information as of the date of this update. Implementation timelines and state-level notifications may vary. SalaryBit is not responsible for any discrepancy arising from state-specific rules or delayed implementation.

5. Insurance Recommendations

Insurance coverage recommendations are generic guidelines based on commonly accepted financial planning principles (e.g., 10–15x annual income for term cover). They are not personalised advice. Actual insurance needs depend on your family situation, liabilities, health status, and risk tolerance. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor or IRDAI-licensed agent.

6. Capital Gains Tax

Share and capital gains tax computations are estimates based on current CBDT guidelines. Foreign tax credit (DTAA) calculations involve treaty interpretation that may differ by jurisdiction and individual circumstance. Consult your CA before filing ITR Schedule FA and Form 67.

About SalaryBit
🇮🇳 Made for Indian Salaried Professionals

Our Mission

SalaryBit was built with a simple mission: give every salaried Indian employee the financial clarity they deserve. Most people receive a salary slip but have no idea where their money actually goes — or how to optimise it.

We believe that understanding your CTC, tax regime options, insurance needs, and employee rights should not require a CA or a finance degree. It should be instant, free, and beautiful.

What We Built

SalaryBit is a single-page, fully browser-based financial intelligence platform covering:

  • In-hand salary calculator — CTC to take-home breakdown
  • Old vs New tax regime comparison — FY 2026-27 slabs
  • Insurance planner — Term, health, and emergency fund
  • Financial roadmap — Priority-ordered action steps
  • Labour Code analyser — New Code on Wages impact
  • Employee rights guide — What to do if fired
  • Share & capital gains tax — Indian, US, and Euro stocks
  • Bonus tools — EMI, PF, gratuity, notice period calculators

Our Promise

Our core calculators are free, need no login, and run in your browser. Optional AI reports are paid and clearly priced before you pay. Your inputs to free calculators stay on your device. We earn revenue through non-intrusive display advertising to keep the service running.

Technology

SalaryBit is built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — lightweight pages with minimal tracking (Google Analytics and ads, see Privacy). It loads fast, works offline after first load, and respects your privacy by design.
