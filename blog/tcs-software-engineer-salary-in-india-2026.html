TCS Software Engineer Salary in India 2026

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is one of the largest IT consulting and business process services companies in the world. If you're looking to join TCS as a software engineer, one of your top considerations will be the salary package. In this article, we'll explore the average salary of TCS software engineers in India in 2026 and provide a comparison table to see the salary difference at various levels.

Average Salary of TCS Software Engineers in India 2026

The average salary of TCS software engineers in India varies based on factors such as location, experience, and the specific role. However, based on data from various sources, including PayScale, Glassdoor, and LinkedIn, here are some approximate average salary ranges for TCS software engineers in India in 2026:

Entry-Level Software Engineers (0-2 years of experience)

According to PayScale, the average salary for an entry-level software engineer at TCS in India is around ₹6.5 lakh (approximately $8,450) per annum. This can vary based on location, with software engineers based in major cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru earning higher salaries compared to those based in smaller cities.

Mid-Level Software Engineers (2-5 years of experience)

For mid-level software engineers, the average salary at TCS in India can range from ₹8 lakh (approximately $10,450) to ₹12 lakh (approximately $15,550) per annum, depending on the specific role and location. Glassdoor reports an average salary of ₹10 lakh (approximately $12,800) per annum for mid-level software engineers at TCS.

Senior-Level Software Engineers (5-8 years of experience)

Senior-level software engineers at TCS in India can earn average salaries ranging from ₹15 lakh (approximately $19,150) to ₹20 lakh (approximately $25,600) per annum. According to LinkedIn, the average salary for senior software engineers at TCS in India is around ₹17 lakh (approximately $21,800) per annum.
