Gratuity calculation formula India

Gratuity is a lump-sum payment made to an employee by the employer at the time of retirement or resignation. It is a payment made to an employee who has completed at least five years of service with the same employer. In India, every employer is required to pay gratuity to their employees as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. In this article, we will discuss the gratuity calculation formula in India and how it works.

Gratuity Calculation Formula in India

The gratuity calculation formula in India is based on the following factors: 1. **Service period**: The employee must have completed at least five years of service with the same employer to be eligible for gratuity. 2. **Last drawn salary**: The gratuity is calculated based on the last drawn salary of the employee. 3. **Monthly average salary**: The gratuity is calculated based on the monthly average salary of the employee. The gratuity calculation formula in India is as follows: Gratuity = (Last drawn salary x Average salary for 26 years) / 15 However, the gratuity is subject to the maximum limit specified by the government, which is 20 lakhs.

How to Calculate Gratuity in India?

To calculate gratuity in India, you need to follow the following steps: 1. **Calculate the average salary for 26 years**: To calculate the average salary for 26 years, you need to add the last drawn salaries for the last 10 months and divide it by 120 (10 months x 12). 2. **Calculate the gratuity**: Once you have calculated the average salary for 26 years, you can calculate the gratuity by multiplying it with the last drawn salary and dividing it by 15. Here's an example to illustrate the gratuity calculation formula: Let's assume an employee has completed 10 years of service with a monthly last drawn salary of Rs 50,000. Average salary for 26 years = (Rs 40,000 + Rs 43,000 + Rs 46,000 + Rs 49,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 50,000) / 10 = Rs 46,000 Gratuity = (Rs 50,000 x Rs 46,000) / 15 = Rs 15,33,333 However, the gratuity is subject to the maximum limit specified by the government, which is 20 lakhs. Therefore, the gratuity payable to the employee would be Rs 20,00,000.

Exclusions from Gratuity

Exclusions from gratuity in India are as follows: 1. **Voluntary retirement**: If an employee retires voluntarily, he is not eligible for gratuity. 2. **Death**: If an employee dies during service, his gratuity is paid to his nominee or dependents. 3. **Redundancy**: If an employee is retrenched or made redundant, he is not eligible for gratuity. 4. **Retirement below 55 years**: If an employee retires below 55 years, he is not eligible for gratuity.
