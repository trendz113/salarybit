HRA Calculation Formula in India

As an employee in India, it's essential to understand how to calculate Household Rent Allowance (HRA) or House Rent Allowance, a tax-exempt allowance provided by employers to cover their employees' housing expenses.

In this article, we'll delve into the HRA calculation formula in India, its components, and provide a salary comparison table to help you understand the amount of HRA you can claim.

Components of HRA Calculation Formula:

The HRA calculation formula in India involves three components:

  • Fair Rent (FR): The maximum rent for a property in the location where the employee resides.
  • Actual Rent (AR): The actual rent paid by the employee for the property.
  • 90% of Basic Salary (BS): The percentage of basic salary that the employee can claim as HRA.

HRA Calculation Formula:

The HRA calculation formula is as follows:

HRA = Min [ (FR - AR), (90% of BS) ]

However, there's an alternative method to calculate HRA, which takes into account the rent paid by the employee:

HRA = Min [ (10% of BS), (FR - AR) ]

Who Can Claim HRA?

HRA can be claimed by salaried individuals who are renting a house or apartment and are not paying home loan. If the employee is paying a home loan, they can claim the interest paid on the loan as tax deductions under Section 24 of the Income Tax Act.
