Gratuity calculation formula India

In India, gratuity is a type of retirement benefit that employers provide to their employees after a specified tenure. It is a sum of money paid to an employee at the time of retirement or resignation to compensate for their loyalty and service to the company. In this article, we will discuss the gratuity calculation formula in India, its eligibility criteria, and the process of claiming gratuity.

Gratuity Eligibility Criteria in India

To be eligible for gratuity in India, an employee must meet the following criteria: * The employee must have been employed with the same employer for a continuous period of not less than five years. * The employee must have completed a specific tenure, which has been notified by the government from time to time. * The employee must have been receiving a salary or wages from the employer at the time of retirement or resignation.

Gratuity Calculation Formula in India

The gratuity calculation formula in India is as follows: * 15 days of last drawn salary for every completed year of service, subject to a maximum of 20 lakhs (2 million rupees). * For employees who have been employed for more than five years, the gratuity amount is calculated at the rate of 17 days of last drawn salary for every completed year of service. * For employees who have been employed for more than 20 years, the gratuity amount is calculated at the rate of 21 days of last drawn salary for every completed year of service. Gratuity Calculation Example

Let's consider an example to understand the gratuity calculation formula in India. Assume that an employee has been working with a company for 15 years and the last drawn salary is Rs. 1,00,000. According to the gratuity calculation formula, the gratuity amount will be calculated as follows: * Completed years of service: 15 years * Last drawn salary: Rs. 1,00,000 * Gratuity amount: 15 days of Rs. 1,00,000 = Rs. 22,50,000

Gratuity Claim Process in India

To claim gratuity in India, an employee must follow the following steps: * Resign or retire from the job. * Submit a written application for gratuity to the employer. * Provide supporting documents, such as service details and last drawn salary proof. * The employer will calculate the gratuity amount based on the gratuity calculation formula and pay it to the employee.

Tax Implications of Gratuity in India

Gratuity received by an employee in India is exempt from tax up to Rs. 10 lakhs (1 million rupees) under Section 10(10)(ii) of the Income-tax Act, 1961. However, gratuity amount exceeding Rs. 10 lakhs will be taxed under the head "Salaries".

Gratuity in Case of Death of the Employee

In case of the death of an employee, the gratuity amount will be paid to the nominee or legal heir of the employee. If the employee has not appointed a nominee, the gratuity amount will be paid to the legal heir as per the laws of succession.

Gratuity in Case of Resignation

In case of resignation, the gratuity amount will be calculated based on the last drawn salary and completed years of service as on the date of resignation. However, the gratuity amount will be paid only if the employee has completed a minimum of five years of service with the employer.
