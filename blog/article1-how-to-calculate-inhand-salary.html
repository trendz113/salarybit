Introduction to In-Hand Salary Calculation in India

Every salaried employee in India faces a significant gap between their Cost to Company (CTC) and their actual in-hand salary. Understanding this gap is crucial for budgeting, tax planning, and salary negotiations. According to AmbitionBox, the average salary for a software engineer in India is around ₹15 lakhs per annum, with an in-hand salary ranging from ₹10 lakhs to ₹12 lakhs per annum. In this article, we will break down the steps to calculate your in-hand salary in India for the financial year 2026-27.

CTC vs. In-Hand Salary: What's the Difference?

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total annual cost your employer incurs for employing you, including your salary, allowances, benefits, and employer contributions to PF and other funds. In-hand salary, on the other hand, is the actual amount credited to your bank account every month after all deductions. According to Glassdoor, the average CTC for a software engineer in India is around ₹18 lakhs per annum, while the in-hand salary is around ₹12 lakhs per annum.

Factors Affecting In-Hand Salary

Several factors can affect your in-hand salary, including the tax regime chosen, salary structure, city of work, and employer-specific benefits. The choice of tax regime, either the Old Regime or the New Regime, can significantly impact the in-hand salary. According to PayScale, the average salary for a software engineer in India can vary significantly based on the city of work, with cities like Bangalore and Pune offering higher salaries than other cities.

The salary structure also plays a crucial role in determining the in-hand salary. A salary structure with a higher proportion of tax-exempt allowances, such as HRA and LTA, can result in a higher in-hand salary. Additionally, employer-specific benefits, such as medical insurance and meal vouchers, can also impact the in-hand salary.

Practical Tips to Increase Your In-Hand Salary

To increase your in-hand salary, you can optimize your salary structure by maximizing tax-exempt allowances, choose the right tax regime, and consider other benefits such as HRA, LTA, and food coupons. You can also negotiate with your employer to include more tax-exempt allowances in your salary structure. According to AmbitionBox, employees who negotiate their salary structure can increase their in-hand salary by up to 10%.

Another way to increase your in-hand salary is to choose the right tax regime. The Old Regime and the New Regime have different tax rates and deductions, and choosing the right regime can result in a higher in-hand salary. What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary?

In-hand salary, on the other hand, is the actual amount credited to your bank account every month after all deductions.

How is in-hand salary calculated in India?

In-hand salary is calculated by subtracting employee deductions, such as PF, professional tax, and TDS, from the gross salary. The gross salary is calculated by subtracting employer PF, gratuity, and other benefits from the CTC.
