The two things your salary slip never mentions

🏥 Medical cover that belongs to the job

Employer health cover typically ends when employment ends, and group cover often has a modest sum insured, commonly around ₹3 to 5 lakh. A fresh personal policy can restart waiting periods, and premiums rise with age.

🛡️ Term life with accidental cover

Cover is usually easier and cheaper to get while you are young and healthy. A common rule of thumb is 10 to 15 times your yearly income. It is only a starting point: your loans, dependants and savings change the number.
