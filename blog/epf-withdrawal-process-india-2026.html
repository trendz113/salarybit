EPF withdrawal process India 2026

The Employee Provident Fund (EPF) is a social security scheme managed by the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. It is a mandatory savings scheme designed for salaried employees, providing them a safety net for their retirement, old age, or any other unforeseen circumstances. Employees in India contribute to their EPF account through their monthly salary, and the employer also contributes to the fund. This article will guide you through the EPF withdrawal process in India 2026, including the rules, eligibility, and required documents for withdrawal. We will also discuss the EPF partial withdrawal rules, EPF online services, and provide a detailed salary table to help you understand the EPF contribution and withdrawal details. ### EPF Contribution and Withdrawal Details at a Glance | Type of Contribution | Amount Contributed by Employee | Amount Contributed by Employer | Total Amount (Monthly) | | --- | --- | --- | --- | | Basic Salary | 12% (Basic + DA + Bonus) | 12% (Basic + DA + Bonus) | 24% (Basic + DA + Bonus) | | Special Drawings (if applicable) | As per the company's policy | As per the company's policy | As per the company's policy | For example, let's consider a sample salary breakdown with basic salary, Dearness Allowance (DA), and Bonus: | Basic Salary | DA | Bonus | Total Salary | | --- | --- | --- | --- | | 30,000 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 45,000 | In this case, the EPF contribution would be 12% of the total salary (Basic + DA + Bonus), which amounts to 5,400 (12% of 45,000). The employer also contributes 12% of the total salary, which amounts to 5,400 (12% of 45,000). Thus, the total EPF contributions per month would be 10,800 (Employee) + 5,400 (Employer) = 16,200. ### Eligibility for EPF Withdrawal in India 2026 To be eligible for EPF withdrawal, you must meet certain conditions, which are: * You should be an EPF member with at least 5 years of continuous service in an establishment with 20 or more employees. * You should have completed 50 years of age or are a widow(er) or have become disabled. * If you have resigned or were terminated, you should have completed a minimum of 5 years of service and have an account balance of at least 100. * If your employer has closed down the establishment, you should have completed a minimum of 1 year of service. ### Required Documents for EPF Withdrawal The required documents for EPF withdrawal include: * Form 19 (PF Withdrawal Application) * A copy of your EPF passbook or account statement * A copy of your Aadhaar card for KYC verification * A cancelled cheque of your bank account * Any other document required as per the EPFO's discretion ### EPF Partial Withdrawal Rules You can partially withdraw from the EPF for specific purposes, such as: * Marriage * Purchase of a house * Higher education * Medical treatment * Repayment of a housing loan The allowed partial withdrawal amount is: * Up to 50% of the employee's account balance for these purposes * The minimum withdrawal amount is 250 * The maximum withdrawal amount is 36,000 ### Using EPF Online Services for Withdrawal You can use online services provided by the EPFO to apply for EPF withdrawal. The online application process involves: * Logging into the EPFO's website (epfindia.gov.in) * Filling out the online application form (Form 19) * Uploading the required documents * Making the payment online * Submitting the application
