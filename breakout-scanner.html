What this flags: BREAKOUT means it has already happened — price closed above the prior 20-bar high on strong volume. That row's "Buy at" price is a real, placeable limit order (a small buffer above the trigger so it fills), and "Sell target" is the exact price to net your profit after fees. WATCH means it hasn't triggered — no order to place yet, just the price level to watch for.

Dip buy is a separate, independent strategy shown for every coin: it's a limit buy placed below the current price, sized so that if the price simply comes back up to where it is right now, you net your target profit — no breakout required. This does not mean you can't lose. Price can keep falling past your dip-buy level instead of bouncing back, and "current price" will have moved by the time your order fills, so treat it as a reference, re-check it before placing the order, and always set a stop-loss below your entry.
