Documents to keep ready

You use these on the official portal. You do not upload them to us.

City / urban property (e-Aasthi)
  • Sale deed registration number from Kaveri (format like DSP-X-XXXXX-YYYY-YY). If registered before 1 April 2004, scanned title documents are uploaded manually.
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC), PDF under 5 MB (required)
  • Photograph of each owner and joint owner, JPG under 200 KB (required)
  • Property photograph taken from outside, showing the front, JPG under 500 KB (required)
  • Aadhaar and the Aadhaar-linked mobile number of each owner, for e-KYC (required)
  • Supporting documents such as property tax receipt, khata certificate/extract or Pahani/mutation order, PDF under 5 MB each (optional or as the portal asks)
Gram panchayat / rural property (e-Swathu)
  • Sale deed registration number from Kaveri (properties registered before 1 April 2004 need manual entry)
  • Aadhaar card of every owner in the deed, for e-KYC, and a registered mobile number (Aadhaar-linked preferred)
  • Property photograph, front view, JPG under 500 KB
  • Postal address details: door/plot number, building name, landmark, area, pincode. Panchayat and village must match your deed exactly.

Based on the official portals as documented in May 2026. Requirements can change, so we confirm the current ones when we help you.
