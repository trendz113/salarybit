EPF withdrawal process India 2026

In India, employees enjoy the benefit of the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), a statutory pension scheme managed by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). EPF is a mandatory contribution made by both the employer and employee, typically 12% each of the basic salary. The fund is designed to provide financial security to employees during retirement or in case of unemployment, medical emergencies, or other unforeseen circumstances. Withdrawal from EPF account is allowed in specific situations, and the process has undergone changes over the years.

Eligibility for EPF Withdrawal

As per the EPFO regulations, EPF members are eligible to withdraw their money under the following circumstances: * After serving the organization for five years or more * Resignation or termination of service, subject to some conditions * Death of the member * Total or Permanent Disability However, withdrawal from EPF is subject to certain restrictions and taxes. If an employee withdraws money before the age of 58, a 10% penalty is levied for non-retirement cases, in addition to income tax.

EPF Withdrawal Process

To withdraw EPF money, you can follow the below steps: ### Online Process 1. Visit the EPFO website ([www.epfo.gov.in](http://www.epfo.gov.in)) and log in using your Universal Account Number (UAN) and password. 2. Click on the "Online Services" tab and select the "Claim" option. 3. Choose the type of claim (e.g., final settlement, advance, or partial withdrawal). 4. Enter the required details, including the claim reason and employer's ID. 5. Upload the necessary documents, such as ID proof, address proof, and bank account details. 6. Submit the claim form and await approval from the EPFO. ### Offline Process To withdraw EPF money through the offline process: 1. Obtain a withdrawal form (Form 19 or Form 31) from the designated EPFO office or download it from the EPFO website. 2. Fill out the form, providing your details, reasons for withdrawal, and employer's ID. 3. Attach the necessary documents, including ID proof, address proof, and bank account details. 4. Submit the form and wait for approval from the EPFO. ### EPF Withdrawal Rules Post 2018 The EPFO regulations have changed over the years. As of 2018, if an employee leaves the organization before serving for five years, they can withdraw a part of the EPF amount without needing to provide proof of new employment. However, this may attract a 1.25% interest rate, instead of the regular 8-9% interest rate. ### Tax on EPF Withdrawal Withdrawal from an EPF account before the age of 58 is taxable as per the Income Tax Act, 1961. However, if the EPF amount is withdrawn after the age of 58, it is exempt from tax. ### How to Get Your EPF Withdrawal Amount If your EPF withdrawal claim is approved, the amount will be directly credited to your bank account. In some cases, you might need to visit the employer's office to obtain the amount.

EPF Withdrawal for Fixed Deposits

In some cases, EPF subscribers can opt for fixed deposits using their EPF corpus. EPFO members can invest in the EPF Scheme 2015, which offers a 8.1% annual interest rate.

Important Dates for EPF Withdrawal

* 5 Years of Service: Minimum service period required to withdraw EPF money * 10% Penalty: Levied for non-retirement cases * 1.25% Interest Rate: Applied for partial withdrawal before 5 years of service * 8.1% Interest Rate: APPLICABLE for EPF Scheme 2015

Documents Required for EPF Withdrawal

* ID Proof * Address Proof * Bank Account Details * Form 19 or Form 31 (withdrawal form) * ID and Proof of Membership (UAN card) ### FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum service period required to withdraw EPF money?

5 years of service with the organization.

Is EPF withdrawal taxable?

If you withdraw money before the age of 58, the amount will be taxable.

How do I get my EPF withdrawal amount?

Your EPF withdrawal amount will be directly credited to your bank account.
