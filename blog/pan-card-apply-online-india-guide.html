PAN card apply online India guide

The Permanent Account Number (PAN) card is an essential document in India, used for various financial transactions, tax purposes, and other official communications. While it's possible to obtain a PAN card offline, applying online is a faster and more convenient method. In this article, we will guide you through the step-by-step process of applying for a PAN card online in India.

Eligibility and Documents Required

To apply for a PAN card online, you must meet the following eligibility criteria:

  • Be a resident of India
  • Be above 18 years of age (for individuals)
  • Be a citizen of India (for non-resident Indians, additional documentation is required)

You will need the following documents to complete the online application:

  • Identity Proof (any one of the following): Aadhaar card number, Driving license number, Passport number, Voter ID card number, Ration card number
  • Address Proof (any one of the following): Bank account number, Bank statement, Ration card, Passport, Utility bill
  • Photograph (in JPEG/PNG format, size not exceeding 100KB)

Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. Visit the official website of the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal (https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/)
  2. Click on the "Services" section and select "Apply for PAN" from the dropdown menu
  3. Choose the category you belong to (Individual, HUF, Partnership Firm, Company, LLP, Trust, etc.) and enter your name, email address, and phone number
  4. Select your identity and address proof documents from the given options
  5. Upload your photograph and other required documents (in JPEG/PNG format, size not exceeding 100KB)
  6. Enter your name and date of birth, and click on "Submit"
  7. Pay the application fee (INR 93.8 + GST) using a valid debit/credit card or net banking
  8. A confirmation message with your application ID and PAN card details will be sent to your registered email address

PAN Card Charges and Taxes

The PAN card application fee is INR 93.8, which includes GST (Goods and Services Tax). You can check the tax rates online before making the payment.

What's Next?

After submitting your online application, the PAN card will be processed, and a Unique Identification Number (UIN) will be generated. You can check the status of your application using the UIN on the official Income Tax Department's website.

Around 6-8 weeks after submitting your application, you will receive your PAN card by post. You can check the status of your application online or by contacting the NSDL (National Securities Depository Limited) customer care helpline at +91 90222 90222.

Important Notes and Updates

Before applying for PAN card online, ensure that you have a valid email address and phone number for communication purposes.

As the PAN card is a sensitive document, avoid sharing it with anyone, and keep it secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Question: What is the PAN card application fee?

The PAN card application fee is INR 93.8 (inclusive of GST).

Question: Can I apply for a PAN card if I'm not a resident of India?

No, to apply for a PAN card, you must be a resident of India. If you're a non-resident Indian, you'll need to provide additional documentation.

Question: How long does it take to get my PAN card?

It takes around 6-8 weeks to receive your PAN card by post after submitting your online application.

Question: Can I check the status of my PAN card application online?

Yes, you can check the status of your application using the UIN on the official Income Tax Department's website, or by contacting the NSDL customer care helpline.

Question: Do I need to submit any documents offline after applying online?

No, you can submit all the required documents online while filling the application form.
