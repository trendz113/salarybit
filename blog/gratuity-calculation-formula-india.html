Gratuity calculation formula India

Gratuity, also known as a retiring allowance or termination gratuity, is a payment made to an employee as a one-time payout on their retirement or resignation. This amount is calculated based on the salary, tenure, and type of employment. In this article, we will delve into the gratuity calculation formula India and provide a comprehensive guide on how to calculate gratuity in India. **What is the gratuity calculation formula in India?** The gratuity calculation formula in India is based on the Employee's Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. According to this act, gratuity is calculated based on the salary and tenure of the employee. The formula is as follows: Gratuity = (15 days' wages for eachcompleted year of service, rounded to the nearest rupee) Where: - 15 days' wages = (Last drawn salary × 15 days) - Eligible service = Completed years of service, rounded to the nearest year **Factors influencing gratuity calculation** Several factors affect the gratuity calculation, including: - **Salary**: The employee's last drawn salary is used to calculate gratuity. This means that the higher the salary, the higher the gratuity amount. - **Service tenure**: The length of service is another crucial factor in gratuity calculation. Employees with longer tenures are entitled to higher gratuity amounts. - **Eligibility**: Gratuity is calculated based on completed years of service, which means that only the time worked is taken into account, not the time on probation. **Gratuity calculation example** Let's consider an example to illustrate the gratuity calculation formula: | Employee Details | | | --- | --- | | Name | John | | Last drawn salary (rupees) | 1,20,000 per month | | Tenure (years) | 10 | | Date of resignation | June 30, 2026 | Using the gratuity calculation formula: 1. This means that you don't have to pay income tax on the gratuity amount received. **How to claim gratuity on resignation?** If you resign from a company, you are eligible to claim gratuity from your employer. Here are the steps to follow: 1. Ensure you have completed at least 5 years of service to be eligible for gratuity. 2. Check your employment contract to see if there's a clause for gratuity on resignation. 3. Provide your employer with a no-objection certificate to enable them to process your gratuity claim. 4. Your employer will calculate the gratuity amount and pay it to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the minimum service tenure required to claim gratuity?

A: You need to have completed at least 5 years of service to be eligible for gratuity.

Q: Is gratuity tax-exempted?

A: Yes, gratuity is a tax-exempt payment, as per the Income Tax Act of 1961.

Q: Can I claim gratuity on resignation?

A: Yes, you can claim gratuity if you have completed at least 5 years of service. Check your employment contract to see if there's a clause for gratuity on resignation.

Q: What details do I need to provide to claim gratuity?

A: You'll need to provide your employer with a no-objection certificate and any other necessary documents to enable them to process your gratuity claim.

Q: How long does it take for my employer to process gratuity?

A: The processing time for gratuity claim may vary depending on the company's policies and procedures. Generally, it may take a few days to a few weeks for your employer to process your claim.
