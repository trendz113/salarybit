Section 01

The First 7 Days

Nobody thinks clearly under grief. This is the order most families actually need to follow — not a legal checklist, a practical one.

DAY 1–2
  • Get the medical certificate of cause of death from the hospital/doctor before the body is moved, if possible.
  • Register the death with the local municipal office and get at least 8–10 original death certificate copies — you will need more than you think.
  • Inform close family and the people listed on page 2.
DAY 3–7
  • Do not rush to close or operate bank accounts — most joint accounts and nominated accounts can still be used by the surviving holder/nominee; ask the bank directly rather than assuming.
  • Call the employer's HR to start the process for gratuity, PF, insurance, and pending salary/leave encashment.
  • Inform every insurance company where a policy exists (life, health, vehicle) — claims have time limits.
  • Do not make any big financial decisions (selling property, investments) in the first month.
WITHIN 1 MONTH
  • Meet the family CA or a financial advisor to map out what needs to be transmitted/claimed and its order.
  • Check for a registered Will with the family lawyer or in the documents listed on page 12.
  • Start transmission of mutual funds, demat holdings, and property as guided by the CA/RM — each has a different, specific form.

This page intentionally avoids exact legal steps, which vary by state and bank — use it as an order of operations, and lean on the professionals listed on page 2.
