Gratuity calculation formula India

Introduction to Gratuity in India

Gratuity, also known as a termination benefit, is an incentive paid to employees when they leave their job. In India, the gratuity is regulated by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. The main purpose of this act is to ensure that employees are paid a decent amount when they retire or leave their job due to redundancy, retirement, or termination. This article will dive into the gratuity calculation formula India, providing you with a clear understanding of the rules and regulations governing gratuity payouts.

Eligibility Criteria for Gratuity in India

To be eligible for gratuity, an employee must have completed at least five years of continuous service with their current employer. If an employee has worked for their employer for less than five years, they are not eligible for gratuity. Additionally, employees in managerial and supervisory positions who earn a monthly salary of Rs. 10,000 or more are exempt from receiving gratuity.

Gratuity Calculation Formula India

The gratuity calculation formula in India is based on the length of service and the last drawn salary of the employee. The formula is as follows: 1. Calculate the average monthly salary: Take the employee's last drawn salary and divide it by 12 to get the average monthly salary. 2. Calculate the gratuity: Multiply the average monthly salary by the number of years of service (rounded up to the nearest year). Gratuity = (Average Monthly Salary * Number of Years of Service) Here's an example to illustrate this: Suppose an employee has completed 10 years of service and their last drawn salary is Rs. 50,000 per month. Their average monthly salary would be: Rs. 50,000 / 12 = Rs. 4,166.67 Gratuity = (Rs. 4,166.67 * 10 years) = Rs. 41,666.70

Gratuity Exemption Limit in India

The gratuity exemption limit in India is Rs. 20 lakhs. This means that employees can claim tax exemption for gratuity up to Rs. 20 lakhs under Section 10(10) of the Income-tax Act. Any gratuity amount exceeding Rs. 20 lakhs will be taxed as per the employee's income tax slab.

Tax Implications of Gratuity in India

As mentioned earlier, gratuity is taxable if it exceeds Rs. 20 lakhs. However, the government has introduced a tax deduction at source (TDS) provision for gratuity. Employers are required to deduct TDS on gratuity payments exceeding Rs. 2.5 lakhs.
