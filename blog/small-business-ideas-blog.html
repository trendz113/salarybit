50 Small Business Ideas You Can Start With Low Investment

A ledger of low-cost businesses anyone can start — freelance skills, home-based production, reselling, consulting, and small shops — each entered with its investment range and realistic monthly income.

Losing a job feels like the ground has shifted under you. But it's also one of those rare moments that forces a question most people never stop to ask: what if I built something of my own instead of waiting to be hired again? You don't need lakhs of rupees or a business degree — you need clarity on what you're good at, a small amount of capital, and the discipline to start small and grow.

Ravi, 34, Bengaluru — laid off from a mid-level corporate job, sleepless with the "what now" question every laid-off professional knows. Within three months he'd turned a modest investment into a small flour-and-grocery counter that now earns more than his last salary — not because he found a secret idea, but because he picked one, tested it small, and stuck with it.

How to read each entry: Investment is the approximate total to get started — equipment, stock, and basic setup. Income is a realistic early-stage monthly range once the business has found its first regular customers, not day one. Pick 3–5 that match your capital and comfort level before you commit to one.

Fastest to Start Earning

Freelance skills and gig work — near-zero investment, income can start within days.

01

Freelance content writing

Write articles, blog posts, or product descriptions for businesses that need regular content. Start on freelance platforms or by directly pitching small local businesses that don't have a writer on staff — a laptop and a portfolio of 3–4 samples is all you need.

Graphic design services

Logos, social media creatives, and packaging design for shops and small brands. A free tool like Canva can get you started before you invest in Photoshop or Illustrator licenses once orders pick up.

₹0–₹15K₹10K–₹30K/mo
03

Video editing

Edit for YouTubers, wedding videographers, or businesses needing reels and ads. Basic setup is a decent laptop and free/trial editing software — most income here comes from repeat clients once you build a fast turnaround reputation.

04

Social media management

Run Instagram and Facebook pages for local shops, restaurants, and clinics who know they need a presence but don't have time for it. Charge a flat monthly retainer per client — 5–6 small clients is a comfortable full income.

Manage day-to-day accounts, GST filing support, and expense tracking for small traders and shop owners who can't afford a full-time accountant. Trust builds slowly here, but retained clients rarely leave.

06

Resume & LinkedIn writing

₹0–₹5K₹6K–₹18K/mo

Notary / document assistance

Voiceover artist

Translation services

Regional language to English (and back) for documents, subtitles, and business communication. Strong local demand for Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and Marathi translation in growing towns.

₹0–₹5K₹8K–₹20K/mo

Virtual assistant

Remote admin support — managing emails, calendars, and data entry — for small business owners and busy professionals abroad or in bigger cities. Fully location-independent work.

Electrician, plumber, or general fix-it services booked through WhatsApp groups and local apartment communities. A basic toolkit and reliability are your biggest assets — repeat calls come fast once residents trust you.

Personal shopper / errand runner

Run errands and do shopping for busy professionals and elderly residents who'll pay a small convenience fee. Works especially well in gated communities and senior-heavy neighbourhoods.

Home-Based Production

Jam, sauces & preserves

Stitching and custom embroidery from home — alterations are quick repeat income, while festive and bridal orders bring in the bigger margins.

Terracotta / pottery craft

Dry fruits & nuts packaging

Consulting & Advisory
