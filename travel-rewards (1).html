Frequently asked questions about credit card reward points in India

How do I redeem HDFC Infinia credit card points for flights? +

Log in to HDFC NetBanking → SmartBuy → Transfer Points → Air India Flying Returns. Transfer at 1:1 ratio. 10,000 HDFC Infinia points = 10,000 Air India miles. Then book award flights at airindia.in. Business class redemptions give the best value — often 3–5× better than cashback. Minimum transfer is usually 2,000 points; miles credit within 3–5 business days.

How does Axis Magnus EDGE Miles work? +

Axis Magnus earns 12 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on travel and 5 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on all other spends. Transfer EDGE Miles to: Air India Flying Returns (1:1), InterMiles (4 EDGE = 5 InterMiles), KrisFlyer (5:4), Marriott Bonvoy (5:3). Log in to edgerewards.axisbank.com to transfer. Dubai business class via InterMiles typically needs 40,000–60,000 miles.

Which Indian credit card gives the best travel rewards in 2025? +

For most frequent flyers: Axis Magnus (best earn rate, 12 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on travel). For high earners with HDFC relationship: HDFC Infinia (5 pts per ₹150, 1:1 Air India). For flexibility: Amex Platinum Travel (points never expire, 20+ transfer partners). For occasional travellers: SBI Elite (10X on international spends, lower fee). Use the free AI tool above to find the best card for your specific spend pattern.

Do HDFC reward points expire? How do I check? +

Yes. HDFC reward points expire 2 years from accrual (Infinia: 3 years). Check via HDFC NetBanking → Cards → Reward Points, or on the HDFC Bank app. To save expiring points: transfer to Air India Flying Returns via SmartBuy before expiry. Alternatively, use on SmartBuy for flight or hotel bookings. Do not let them lapse — even small amounts are worth saving.

Is cashback or travel redemption better for Indian credit cards? +

Travel redemption is almost always 2–5× better. Example — HDFC Infinia: cashback gives ₹0.50/point. Same points as Air India miles for Business Class give ₹1.50–₹2.50/point in value. 50,000 points = ₹2,500 cashback OR ₹7,500–₹12,000 in Business Class flight value. The gap is biggest for international Business and First Class, where market ticket prices are very high but mile costs don't increase proportionally.

How many Air India Flying Returns miles for a free flight? +

Approximate Air India Flying Returns award requirements: Domestic economy 5,000–12,000 miles | Domestic business 15,000–25,000 miles | Mumbai–Dubai economy 20,000–30,000 miles | Mumbai–Dubai business 45,000–60,000 miles | Mumbai–London economy 40,000–55,000 miles | Mumbai–London business 70,000–90,000 miles | Mumbai–New York business 90,000–1,20,000 miles. Rates vary by season and availability — check airindia.in for current award charts.

How to maximise Indian credit card reward points for travel (2025 guide)

Most Indian credit cardholders redeem their reward points for cashback or merchandise — getting roughly 0.25 to 0.50 paisa per point. But the same points, transferred to the right airline loyalty program, can be worth 2–5× more. HDFC Infinia points transferred to Air India Flying Returns at a 1:1 ratio, for example, can unlock Business Class flights that would otherwise cost ₹40,000–₹80,000 per sector. This guide explains everything you need to know.

HDFC credit card reward points — how to use them for flights

HDFC Bank credit cards are among the most popular in India. The Infinia, Regalia, and Diners Black cards earn reward points on every transaction. HDFC Infinia earns 5 reward points per ₹150 spent — that's one of the best earn rates in India. These points can be transferred to Air India Flying Returns at a 1:1 ratio via HDFC SmartBuy, or redeemed directly on SmartBuy for flights at roughly 0.50 paisa per point.

The key insight: never redeem HDFC points for merchandise or cashback. A flight redemption via Air India miles gives you 1.5–2.5 paisa per point in value — 3–5× better. If you have 50,000 HDFC Infinia points, cashback gives you ₹1,250. Transferred to Air India and used for a Delhi–Mumbai business class upgrade, those same points could be worth ₹6,000–₹8,000.

Axis Magnus EDGE Miles — India's best travel credit card explained

The Axis Magnus is widely considered India's best travel credit card for frequent flyers in 2025. It earns 12 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on travel-related spends (flights booked through Axis Travel EDGE, hotels, and travel agencies) and 5 EDGE Miles per ₹200 on everything else. EDGE Miles can be transferred to multiple airline and hotel partners: Air India Flying Returns (1:1), InterMiles (4:5 — meaning 4 EDGE Miles become 5 InterMiles), Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer (5:4), and Marriott Bonvoy (5:3).

For a Business Class ticket to Dubai using InterMiles, you typically need around 40,000–50,000 miles. A cardholder spending ₹1,00,000/month on the Axis Magnus earns approximately 25,000–35,000 EDGE Miles per month — enough for a free Business Class ticket every 1–2 months.

Air India Flying Returns — complete guide to redeeming miles

Air India Flying Returns is India's most accessible frequent flyer program for credit card point transfers. HDFC Infinia, HDFC Diners Black, Axis Magnus, SBI Elite, and several other Indian cards transfer directly to Flying Returns. Award redemptions cover Air India's entire network — domestic routes, Middle East, Europe, USA, and partner airline flights.

Typical award rates: domestic economy from 5,000 miles; Mumbai–Dubai economy from 20,000 miles; Mumbai–London business class from 50,000–70,000 miles. Flying Returns miles expire after 36 months of inactivity — earning or redeeming even one mile resets the clock.

InterMiles — how Indian credit cards transfer to this program

InterMiles (formerly Jet Airways Privilege) is a standalone loyalty currency accepted by multiple airlines. Axis Magnus, HDFC Diners Black, and several co-branded cards transfer points to InterMiles. Miles can be redeemed on Etihad Airways, Jet2, and other partners for flights, or on hotel bookings. The program is particularly useful for Middle East and Europe routes via Etihad.

Comparing cashback vs travel redemption — which is better?

For almost every premium Indian credit card, travel redemption beats cashback by 2–5×. Here's a quick comparison for HDFC Infinia:

Cashback redemption: 1 point = ₹0.50 | 50,000 points = ₹2,500 cashback

Travel redemption via Air India: 1 point = ~₹1.50–₹2.50 in flight value | 50,000 points = ₹7,500–₹12,500 in Business Class flights

The gap is even larger for Business Class and international routes, where the market price of a ticket is high but the miles cost is the same as economy. Always use our free tool above to calculate your specific card's redemption value before choosing cashback.

Do Indian credit card reward points expire?

Yes — most Indian credit card reward points expire, and this is one of the biggest reasons people lose value. HDFC reward points expire 2 years from accrual (Infinia: 3 years). Axis EDGE Miles expire 3 years from earning. Amex Membership Rewards points never expire as long as your account is active — one of Amex's biggest advantages.

Airline miles also expire: Air India Flying Returns miles expire after 36 months of no activity. InterMiles expire after 18 months of inactivity. Set calendar reminders every 6 months to check your balances. Our AI tool above can help you identify expiring points and the best way to use them before they lapse.
